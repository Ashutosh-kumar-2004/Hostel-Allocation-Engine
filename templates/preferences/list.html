How Your Preferences Are Allocated (M6 Solver Guidelines)

1. Priority Optimization

The mathematical solver attempts to assign your Rank #1 choice first. If your top hostel/room type is full, it cascades to Rank #2 and Rank #3 without penalty.

2. Mutual Roommate Pairing

If both you and your requested roommate select each other, you form a Verified Mutual Pair. The solver co-allocates both of you into the same shared room.

3. Objective Tie-Breaking

When demand exceeds available beds, priority is resolved objectively by Distance from campus (>50km), Academic Merit (CGPA), and verified eligibility tier.
